Ohm's Law Formulas

Voltage: V = I × R
Current: I = V ÷ R
Resistance: R = V ÷ I
Power: P = V × I
Power: P = I² × R
Power: P = V² ÷ R

These formulas apply to basic DC calculations and suitable resistive loads. AC circuits involving power factor, reactive components or non-sinusoidal waveforms require additional calculations.
