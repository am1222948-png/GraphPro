Formulas Used

Electrical Power Formulas

Apparent Power = Voltage × Current
Real Power = Voltage × Current × Power Factor
Kilowatts = Watts ÷ 1000
Energy (kWh) = Kilowatts × Hours
Cost = Energy × Electricity Rate

For AC circuits, real power depends on the power factor. This calculator uses the entered voltage and current as RMS values. It is an estimate and does not include additional utility fees or taxes.
